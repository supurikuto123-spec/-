Frequently Asked Questions

This page covers questions about how Sutemeado temporary email service works, how to use it, security, storage periods, and more.

About the Service

What is a temporary/disposable email address?

A temporary or disposable email address is an email address that can be used for a certain period and then discarded. With Sutemeado, simply visiting the site in your browser generates a random mailbox ID, and a temporary email address in the format xxxxxx@sutemeado.com is automatically assigned. It is specialized for receiving emails and requires no personal information such as registration, real name, or phone number.

Why do many people use temporary email?

Temporary email is often used in situations where you don't want to expose your real email address too much. For example: overseas services you just want to try, sites with unclear trustworthiness, or forms where entering an email might result in a flood of advertising emails.

Is it free? Will it become paid in the future?

Currently, Sutemeado is completely free to use, with no registration fees or monthly charges. While operational costs may be covered by advertising revenue, there are no plans to make basic functionality paid.

How to Use

What is the basic flow of usage?

1) Visit sutemeado.com in your browser
2) Copy the displayed temporary email address
3) Paste it into the email field of the service you want to try
4) Wait a few seconds to minutes for the email to arrive
5) Click on the received email to view its contents

Does the email address change every time?

No, you can continue using the same address. Sutemeado uses a password-based access system, so you can access your mailbox from anywhere, regardless of browser cookies or device.

How often are emails automatically updated?

The inbox automatically refreshes approximately every 5 seconds. If you keep the screen open, new emails will appear in near real-time.

Can I use it from mobile/smartphone?

Yes, it is available from smartphones, tablets, and PCs. Simply open sutemeado.com in your browser to use the service.

Security & Privacy

Is Sutemeado safe? Does it support HTTPS?

Sutemeado supports HTTPS (TLS) encrypted communication, protecting data transmitted between your browser and our servers.

How much anonymity is guaranteed?

Temporary email prioritizes email address anonymity. No real name, phone number, or address is required—simply accessing the browser is enough. However, minimal technical information such as IP addresses may be temporarily recorded.

Can others see my emails?

If someone knows both your email address and password, they could potentially access your inbox. Sutemeado mitigates this by requiring both a random string address and a set password.

⚠️ Important
Do not share your password with others.
Can I use it for important accounts (banks, main SNS)?

We do not recommend using temporary email for important accounts that require password resets. If you cannot access the mailbox later, account recovery may become difficult.

⚠️ Warning
Using temporary email for banks or main SNS accounts may lead to trouble such as "cannot view emails" or "cannot reset password."

Storage Period & Data Deletion

How long are emails stored?

Emails are stored for 30 days. Saved emails are kept for 30 days, while unsaved emails are automatically deleted 30 days after receipt.

💡 Tip
Press the "Save" button for important emails to ensure 30-day retention.
Can deleted emails be restored?

Once deleted, emails cannot be restored. We do not provide individual email recovery from backups due to the nature of temporary email services.

Does the address itself have an expiration?

The email address itself can be accessed anytime as long as you know the password. However, addresses that have not received emails for over 30 days may be deleted for system load reduction.

💡 Tip
To maintain your address, receive at least one email within 30 days.

Troubleshooting

What to check when emails don't arrive

Check: whether there are extra spaces when copying the address, if the destination address is correct, if the sender blocks temporary emails, and if enough time has passed (try the refresh button).

The email body is blank. Is it broken?

For HTML emails, external images or stylesheets may be blocked, or layouts dependent on JavaScript may cause display issues. Some emails may appear blank or broken.

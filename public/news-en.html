Mail Delivery Issue Resolved

We have identified and resolved an issue where emails from some external mail services were not being delivered.

The issue was caused by a lack of STARTTLS support on our server, which is required by many email providers.

We have now enabled STARTTLS and restored normal mail delivery. We sincerely apologize for any inconvenience caused.
